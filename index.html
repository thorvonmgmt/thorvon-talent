What We Do

Four core services built around your growth.

01

Account Management

We manage your social media accounts and help maintain a consistent, professional online presence.

02

Profile Assistance

When needed, we assist with running your profiles, coordinating content, and managing day-to-day social media activities.

03

Dedicated Talent Manager

Every represented creator has a dedicated manager who works on their behalf to identify, negotiate, and pursue suitable brand deals and commercial opportunities.

04

Support for New IPs

When creators develop a new intellectual property (IP), original content format, show, podcast, or creative venture, we support its development and help explore relevant opportunities.
