      Privacy Policy & Data Consent

      How Thorvon Talent and Thorvon Media protect your information.

      1. Purpose and Data Collection

      Thorvon Talent operates as a business division of Thorvon Media. When you interact with our website, submit a creator application, request a brand collaboration, or apply for an employment vacancy, we collect relevant information (such as your name, contact information, social profile URLs, and resume links) solely to evaluate and process your request.

      2. Protection of Applicant Data & Resumes

      Candidate resumes, contact details, and application materials are strictly restricted to authorized recruitment and leadership personnel at Thorvon Media. We never sell, rent, or publicly display applicant information or portfolios without your explicit consent.

      3. Creator Confidentiality

      Information submitted by content creators regarding current audience statistics, proprietary brand deals, rates, and personal contact handles is treated under strict confidentiality. We do not distribute your private media kit or contact details to third parties without prior consultation.

      4. Brand Inquiries & Non-Disclosure

      Brand campaign briefs, budget indications, and proposed product launch details shared through our forms are handled confidentially by our business development team.

      5. Data Retention & Inquiries

      You may request the deletion or update of any submitted details at any time by contacting us directly at thorvonmedia@gmail.com.
